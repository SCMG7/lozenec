# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 16, 2026**

RentMate ("we", "our", "us") operates the RentMate mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

## 1. Data We Collect

- **Account information:** email address, full name
- **Property data:** property name, address, type, pricing configuration
- **Reservation data:** guest names, check-in/out dates, payment amounts, booking sources
- **Guest data:** names, phone numbers, email addresses, nationality, ID numbers, notes
- **Expense data:** categories, amounts, dates, descriptions
- **Device information:** device type, operating system version, app version
- **Usage data:** feature interactions and navigation patterns (anonymized)

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To provide and maintain the App's functionality
- To manage your account and property data
- To send password reset emails and service notifications
- To generate analytics, reports, and financial summaries
- To improve the App experience

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- Your data is stored in secure PostgreSQL databases hosted on Railway (EU/US regions)
- All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 12
- Authentication uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT)

## 4. Data Sharing

- **We do not sell your personal data to third parties**
- We may share data with service providers who assist in operating the App (hosting, email delivery)
- We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Object** to processing of your data
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

## 6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please email **support@rentmate.app**. We will process your request within 30 days.

## 7. Data Retention

- Account data is retained for as long as your account is active
- Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
- Anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or email.

## 10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: **support@rentmate.app**
